# 6. Content rules

- Only show public or team-approved material. No unannounced assets, story, or specs.
- Work under NDA: generic description, no client names or logos, unless cleared.
- Collaborative work: describe my part only; credit others.
- Animation of someone else's art: I am the animator, not the artist. Credit the artist in `credits`; never use the `artist` role on it.
- Music and drawing are hobbies: they live under "Hobbies", never in the headline or presented as professional work.
- Songs licensed to others: music entry + `usedIn`, no composer role on that game.
- Be honest about authorship: never claim a skill only shown in AI-built work.
- Vibe-coded work is `ai-built`: I am director/designer, not programmer. See the authorship table in [section 2](2-content-model.md).
